Week 1 Convolutions & Filters Convolutions
Session 1 — Theory
What is a convolution? Sliding kernel intuition. Edge detection: Sobel, Prewitt. Sharpening and blur kernels.
Session 2 — Lab
Implement 2D convolution from scratch with NumPy. Apply Sobel, Gaussian blur. Visualize feature maps. Compare with OpenCV's filter2D.
Students leave able to manually apply and explain any 3×3 kernel.
Week 2 Perceptrons & the Neural Network Idea Neural networks
Session 1 — Theory
Biological neuron analogy. The perceptron: weights, bias, activation (sigmoid, ReLU). From one neuron to a layer. Loss functions: MSE, cross-entropy (intuition only).
Session 2 — Lab
Build a single-layer perceptron in NumPy that classifies two-class toy data. Visualize the decision boundary.
Key message: a neural network is a chain of linear algebra + nonlinearities.
Week 3 Multi-Layer Networks & Training Neural networks
Session 1 — Theory
Hidden layers: why depth helps. Forward pass as matrix multiplications. Backpropagation intuition (chain rule, no deep derivations). Gradient descent: learning rate, epochs, mini-batches.
Session 2 — Lab
Introduce PyTorch. Reimplement week 2's network using nn.Linear. Train on a simple dataset, plot loss curves. Compare learning rates.
Students get comfortable with PyTorch's training loop: forward → loss → backward → step.
Week 4 Convolutional Neural Networks CNNs
Session 1 — Theory
Conv layers as learned filters. Pooling (max, average). Receptive field. The pattern: Conv → ReLU → Pool → Flatten → FC. Overfitting: dropout and data augmentation (conceptually).
Session 2 — Lab
Build a small CNN in PyTorch. Train on MNIST. Visualize learned filters in the first layer. Achieve ~98% accuracy.
Connecting week 1 (hand-crafted kernels) to week 4 (learned kernels) is the "aha" moment.
Week 5 Classic Architectures & Transfer Learning CNNs
Session 1 — Theory
LeNet → AlexNet → VGG: what changed and why. ImageNet context. Transfer learning: freeze early layers, retrain the head. Why this works (features are reusable).
Session 2 — Lab
Load a pretrained VGG or ResNet from torchvision. Fine-tune on a small custom dataset (or CIFAR-10 subset). Compare training from scratch vs. transfer.
Students see that real practitioners rarely train from scratch — a very practical lesson.
Week 6 Project Week — MNIST Digit Classifier Project
Session 1 — Guided work
Students design and implement their own CNN for MNIST. Experiment with architecture choices, hyperparameters, and augmentations. Instructor circulates to advise.
Session 2 — Presentations
Each student/group presents: their architecture, training curves, accuracy, and one thing that surprised them. Peer discussion.
Deliverable: a trained model + short write-up explaining design decisions.
Week 7 Review & Exam Preparation Review
Session 1 — Concept review
Recap: convolutions → perceptrons → backprop → CNNs → architectures. Common exam question types: trace a forward pass, compute output dimensions, explain a design choice.
Session 2 — Q&A / Practice
Students bring questions. Work through practice problems together. Preview exam format.
