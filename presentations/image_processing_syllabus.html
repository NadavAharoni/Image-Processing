Week 1 Convolutions & Filters Convolutions
Session 1 — Theory
What is a convolution? Sliding kernel intuition. Edge detection: Sobel, Prewitt. Sharpening and blur kernels.
Session 2 — Lab
Implement 2D convolution from scratch with NumPy. Apply Sobel, Gaussian blur. Compare with OpenCV's filter2D.
Students leave able to manually apply and explain any 3×3 kernel.
Week 2 Perceptrons & the Neural Network Idea Neural networks
Session 1 — Theory
Biological neuron analogy. Weights, bias, activation functions (sigmoid, ReLU). From one neuron to a layer. Loss functions: cross-entropy intuition.
Session 2 — Lab
Build a single-layer perceptron in NumPy. Classify two-class toy data. Visualize the decision boundary.
Key message: a neural network is a chain of linear algebra + nonlinearities.
Week 3 Training — Backprop & Gradient Descent Neural networks
Session 1 — Theory
Hidden layers and why depth helps. Forward pass as matrix multiplications. Backprop intuition (chain rule, no deep derivations). Learning rate, epochs, mini-batches.
Session 2 — Lab
Introduce PyTorch. Reimplement week 2's perceptron using nn.Linear. Train on a simple dataset, plot loss curves. Compare learning rates.
Students get fluent with the PyTorch training loop: forward → loss → backward → step.
Week 4 Convolutional Neural Networks CNNs
Session 1 — Theory
Conv layers as learned filters. Pooling (max, avg). Receptive field. Pattern: Conv → ReLU → Pool → FC. Overfitting: dropout and data augmentation.
Session 2 — Lab
Build a small CNN in PyTorch. Train on MNIST for quick success, then on CIFAR-10. Visualize first-layer learned filters.
"Aha" moment: week 1's hand-crafted kernels vs. week 4's learned kernels — same idea, now optimized automatically.
Week 5 Classic Architectures + Intro to Generative Models CNNs Generative revised
Session 1 — Architectures
LeNet → AlexNet → VGG: the story of depth. Transfer learning: freeze early layers, retrain the head. Brief demo with torchvision pretrained model.
Session 2 — Generative intro
What does "generative" mean? Autoencoders: encode → latent space → decode. VAE intuition: smooth latent space enables sampling. GAN idea: generator vs. discriminator, the adversarial game. No code yet — build the mental model.
Narrative arc to share: GANs (2014) → StyleGAN → Diffusion models → Stable Diffusion. Students already know the end of this story.
Week 6 Project Week — Build & Present Project revised
Session 1 — Guided work
Students choose track A or B. Track A: CIFAR-10 classifier, push accuracy as high as possible, try transfer learning. Track B: DC-GAN on CIFAR-10 or a custom dataset (e.g. CelebA faces). Instructor circulates.
Session 2 — Presentations
Each student/group presents: architecture, training curves, results, and one thing that surprised them. GAN groups show generated samples. Discussion of what worked and why.
Track B deliverable: a grid of generated images is far more striking to show than a confusion matrix — great for motivation.
Week 7 Review & Exam Preparation Review
Session 1 — Concept recap
Full arc: convolutions → perceptrons → backprop → CNNs → architectures → generative models. Typical exam questions: trace a forward pass, compute output dims, explain a design choice.
Session 2 — Q&A / Practice
Students bring questions. Work through practice problems. Preview exam format.
